# Feasibility of Grid-Scale Deployment - Interim Assessment

## Summary

Pilot installations completed between 2024 and 2026 demonstrate that the approach works at pilot
scale today, though every deployment reviewed here remained under 40 MW. Scaling beyond that band
has not been demonstrated outside laboratory conditions.

## Findings

Across the eleven pilots surveyed, availability exceeded 94% after the first six months of
operation. Two sites reported cold-start latency falling by roughly 40% year over year in tested
clusters, which the operators attribute to firmware maturity rather than to any hardware change.

Cost data submitted by operators was inconsistent and is not reproduced here; readers should treat
vendor-supplied figures with caution until the disclosure standard is finalised.

## Method

Sites self-reported against a common template. Eleven of the fourteen invited operators returned
usable data; the three that did not are excluded rather than imputed.
