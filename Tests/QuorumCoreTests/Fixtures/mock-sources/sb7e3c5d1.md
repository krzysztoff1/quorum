Cost Curves for Grid-Scale Storage, 2019-2026

Abstract

We assemble reported unit costs from 214 disclosed procurements across nine regions. Unit cost
declined year over year across every region in the sample, with a compound annual decline of 11.4%
between 2019 and 2026.

Regional variation

The spread between the cheapest and the most expensive region reached a factor of two in 2026.
Regions with the lowest reported costs are also those carrying direct grid subsidies, and once
subsidies are netted out the regional spread narrows to roughly 20%.

Limitations

Disclosure is voluntary and the sample skews toward large procurements. We make no claim that the
observed decline continues past the end of the sample window.
