General-purpose AI model obligations

Providers of general-purpose AI models must draw up and keep up to date technical documentation of the model, including its training and testing process. They must also provide information and documentation to downstream providers who integrate the model into their AI systems.

Providers must put in place a policy to comply with Union copyright law and publish a sufficiently detailed summary of the content used for training the model, following a template supplied by the AI Office.

Models with systemic risk

A general-purpose AI model is presumed to have systemic risk when the cumulative amount of computation used for its training is greater than 10^25 floating-point operations. Providers of such models must additionally perform model evaluations, assess and mitigate systemic risks, and report serious incidents to the AI Office.
