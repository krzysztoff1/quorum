AI Act application timeline

The AI Act entered into force on 1 August 2024, twenty days after its publication in the Official Journal of the European Union. Most of its provisions apply from 2 August 2026.

The obligations for providers of general-purpose AI models apply from 2 August 2025. Models placed on the market before that date have until 2 August 2027 to comply with them.

  • 2 February 2025: prohibitions on unacceptable-risk AI practices apply.
  • 2 August 2025: governance rules and obligations for general-purpose AI models apply.
  • 2 August 2026: the bulk of the regulation, including high-risk system rules, applies.
